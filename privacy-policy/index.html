Privacy policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This is a general policy written in plain language. It is not legal advice.

Who this covers

I am Mark Lawrence Polidario, an independent GoHighLevel specialist. This policy describes this portfolio website, a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not cover sample pages that show fictional businesses.

What this site collects

This site has no forms. It does not set cookies of its own. It does not run analytics or other tracking scripts. You can read every page without creating an account or sending me any information.

How you can contact me

The only ways to contact me from this site are the email link (marklpolidario@gmail.com) and my Upwork profile.

Email

If you email me, I receive whatever you choose to send. That often includes your name, email address, business details, and your message. I use that information only to reply and to discuss a project with you. I do not sell it. I keep it only as long as I need it for that conversation and any work that comes from it, and then I delete it.

GitHub Pages

GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub, as the host, may log visitor IP addresses for security. That logging is covered by GitHub's general privacy statement. I do not receive those logs through this site.

Upwork

If you message me or hire me on Upwork, that happens on Upwork's site, not on this one. Upwork's own privacy policy applies there: Upwork Privacy Policy.

If I add a calendar or form later

This site does not include a booking calendar or a contact form. If I add one later, for example a GoHighLevel calendar or form, I will update this policy before it collects any information.

Access and deletion

You can ask me what information I still have from your emails, or ask me to delete it. Email marklpolidario@gmail.com and I will reply.

Children

This site is for business owners. It is not meant for children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has emailed me, write to marklpolidario@gmail.com and I will delete that information.

Changes

I may update this policy when the site changes. When I do, I will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. The version published here is the current one.
