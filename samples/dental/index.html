How it works

From first click to follow-up, handled automatically

This is the GoHighLevel setup behind the page: a calendar, a workflow and a few text templates.

  1. Book online

    The patient picks a time on the calendar. The slot is blocked instantly, so no double bookings.

  2. Instant confirmation

    A text and email confirm the visit and include the arrival details and a reschedule link.

  3. Smart reminders

    Reminders go out ahead of the visit. The patient can reply to confirm or ask to move the time.

  4. Review request

    After a completed visit, a friendly message asks for feedback. The workflow marks the contact in the pipeline.

Good to know

Common questions

What happens at a first visit?

You meet the team, we review your health history, do an exam and cleaning, and talk through a plan. Nothing is done without your say-so.

Do you accept my insurance?

Add your provider on the booking form and our team will confirm coverage with you before the visit. (Clinic: list accepted providers here.)

Can I change my appointment?

Yes. Every confirmation and reminder text includes a link to reschedule or cancel.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Tell us in the booking form or reply to our text and we will find the earliest slot. For severe swelling or trouble breathing, contact emergency services.

How is my information used?

Only to arrange and remind you about your care. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP.
