How it works

Follower to client, on autopilot

This is the GoHighLevel funnel behind the page. Every step runs in the background while you coach.

  1. Sign up

    A short application form captures name, email, phone and goal, and tags the contact as a challenge member.

  2. Warm-up sequence

    Email and SMS messages send workouts, tips and nudges on a schedule.

  3. Book a consultation

    Engaged members get an invite to book on your calendar, with reminders so they show up.

  4. Choose a package

    After the call, a payment link starts the package and a check-in workflow begins.

Is the challenge really free?

Yes, signing up to the challenge costs nothing. Coaching packages are optional and priced separately. (Coach: confirm this matches your offer.)

What if I miss a day?

Pick up where you left off. Reminders help, and you can reply to a message if you need a hand.

Do I have to book a consultation?

No. The consultation is for people who want personal coaching after the challenge.

How do I stop messages?

Reply STOP to texts or use the unsubscribe link in any email.
