Behind the scenes

Messages that bring shoppers back

GoHighLevel connects to your store and sends the right message at the right time. Here are two of the flows.

Abandoned cart

  1. Trigger

    Cart left behind

    Your store tells GHL a cart was started but not paid.

  2. After a short wait

    Friendly email

    A reminder shows what is still in the cart.

  3. If no purchase

    SMS nudge

    A short text with a link back to the cart.

  4. Stop

    Order placed

    The flow ends and thank-you messages begin.

After the order

  1. Order placed

    Thank-you message

    Order confirmation with care tips.

  2. Delivered

    Check-in

    A quick note to make sure everything arrived well.

  3. Later

    Review request

    An easy link to share feedback.

  4. Happy customer

    Referral invite

    Invite them to share the shop with a friend.

Timings are adjustable in the workflow builder.
