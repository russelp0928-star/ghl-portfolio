FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Do I need my own GoHighLevel account?

Yes, you'll need an account. If you don't have one yet, we can talk through the options on the strategy call, and I can set it up with you.

What happens on the free strategy call?

We talk about your business, how leads find you, and where follow-up breaks down. You leave with a clear idea of what I'd build and in what order.

Yes. I can audit what you have, clean up the pipelines and workflows, and fix automations that aren't firing as expected.

Yes. I build with clear names and structure, and I walk you or your team through how it works. You can also keep me on for ongoing changes.

It depends on what you need. After the strategy call, I'll send a clear scope and quote before any work starts.
